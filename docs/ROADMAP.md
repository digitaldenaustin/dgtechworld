# Digital Den Austin Roadmap

## Phase 1 — Storefront foundation
- Next.js storefront
- Product catalog and product pages
- Current inventory
- Mobile-first design and SEO

## Phase 2 — Supabase backend
- Products, product images and categories tables
- Admin authentication
- Row Level Security
- Storage bucket for product images

## Phase 3 — Secure admin dashboard
- Add/edit/archive products
- Available, pending and sold statuses
- Private cost, IMEI/serial and profit fields
- Testing checklist and condition grading

## Phase 4 — Customer marketplace features
- Search and filters
- Favorites and saved searches
- Purchase requests and pickup scheduling
- Reviews and trust profile

## Phase 5 — Commerce and automation
- Online payments
- Shipping and labels
- Trade-in offers
- Analytics and notifications
